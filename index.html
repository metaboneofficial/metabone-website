FAQ

Common questions

How is plant-based calcium better than calcium carbonate supplements? ▾
Calcium in food matrices is absorbed more gently by the gut, resulting in fewer side effects like bloating and constipation. High-dose calcium carbonate supplements have also been associated with a modest increase in kidney stone risk in susceptible individuals — whereas food-based calcium has a much more favourable safety profile.
Why does MetaBone also include Vitamin D? ▾
Calcium absorption in the intestine is tightly regulated by Vitamin D. Without adequate Vitamin D, a significant portion of ingested calcium passes through unabsorbed. By fortifying every MetaBone product with Vitamin D, we ensure the calcium you consume actually reaches your bones.
Can children use MetaBone products? ▾
Yes. The Multigrain Flour and Wellness Drink Premix are suitable for the whole family, including children. The food formats make it easy for children to get calcium without having to take tablets or supplements.
How much more calcium does MetaBone have compared to milk? ▾
Full-fat milk provides approximately 120 mg of calcium per 100 ml. MetaBone delivers 210 mg of calcium per serving — drawn entirely from plant sources. That is approximately 75% more calcium per serving, without any dairy.
Is MetaBone safe for people who are lactose intolerant? ▾
Absolutely. MetaBone uses no dairy ingredients whatsoever. The calcium comes entirely from Himalayan plant sources, making it an ideal solution for the many Indians who are lactose intolerant or simply prefer to avoid dairy.
What is the source of the plant-based calcium in MetaBone? ▾
The calcium in MetaBone products is derived from Himalayan botanical sources — the same high-altitude plants and herbs that Subhag Himalayan Resources has supplied to international B2B customers for years. The specific formulation is proprietary.
